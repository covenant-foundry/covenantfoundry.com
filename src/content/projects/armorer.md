Armorer is a focused reader for the King James Version of the Bible, built around a simple premise: Scripture should be the main thing on the screen.

Its typography, navigation, and controls recede once a passage is open. Readers can move from a quick lookup into sustained reading without working around an editorial layer.

The project is maintained as a small, durable web application. Changes are guided by whether they make the text easier to reach, read, and share.
